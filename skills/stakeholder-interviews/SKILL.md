# Stakeholder interviews

Most projects fail on misalignment, not on design. Talk to the people who can kill, fund or ship the work, one by one, before anything is designed.

## Who

Product, engineering, data, marketing, sales or support, legal or compliance, and whoever owns the budget. In regulated products, always compliance.

## Questions to cover

1. What does success look like for you, and how would we measure it?
2. What has been tried before, and why didn't it work?
3. What constraints are fixed (technical, legal, brand, timeline) and which are negotiable?
4. What do you believe about our users that we haven't proven?
5. What worries you most about this project?
6. Who else should I talk to?

## Synthesis

- **Goals** each stakeholder named, and where they conflict.
- **Constraints**, labelled fixed or negotiable, with who said so.
- **Assumptions map:** each belief about users, rated by how important it is and how much evidence exists. High importance plus low evidence is what research should test first.
- **Risks** and who owns them.

## Output

A one page alignment summary to share back with everyone, plus the assumptions map. Disagreements are named plainly, without attributing blame.

## Don't

- Don't treat a stakeholder's assumption about users as a finding.
