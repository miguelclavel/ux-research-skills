# Journey map

Map what happens today, from evidence, before mapping what should happen.

## Steps

1. **Scope.** One persona or segment, one scenario, a clear start and end ("from renewal notice to new policy").
2. **Stages** in the person's words, not the company's org chart (four to seven).
3. **For each stage:** what they do, what they're thinking (with a real quote), how they feel (a simple high, neutral, low line), the channels and touchpoints, and the pain points.
4. **Evidence.** Every pain point has a source: participant ids, an analytics drop off, a support ticket theme. Mark anything assumed.
5. **Moments that matter.** The two or three points where people decide to continue or leave.
6. **Opportunities** framed as "How might we..." questions tied to specific pain points, not solutions.
7. **Behind the scenes (optional):** the systems and teams behind each touchpoint, which turns it toward a service blueprint.

## Output

A table with stages as columns and the rows above, then the moments that matter and the opportunity questions. Keep it readable in one screen.

## Don't

- Don't map the ideal journey and call it research. Don't smooth over the low points.
